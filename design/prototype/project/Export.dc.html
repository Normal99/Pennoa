Kinematics — summary Physics · pp. 9–14
Key equations
v = u + at s = ut + ½at² v² = u² + 2as s = ½(u + v)t
Ideas to remember
  • The equations hold only when acceleration is constant.
  • The area under a velocity–time graph is the displacement.
  • Choose the equation that skips the quantity you don't know.
Worked example (from your notes)

Car: u = 12 m/s, a = 3 m/s², t = 5 s → v = 27 m/s, s = 97.5 m.

Practice
  1. A bike slows from 10 m/s to rest in 4 s. Find its acceleration.
  2. How far does it travel while braking?
  3. Sketch its velocity–time graph and shade the displacement.
